### What the patch does
`docs/phoenix/tracing/how-to-tracing/cost-tracking.mdx` lists the providers whose model prices Phoenix ships built in. Phoenix recently added Mistral AI pricing (#16880). The audit found that this list never mentions Mistral, and that four other providers already in the price table are missing too.

The patch adds five bullets, kept in the list's existing alphabetical tail:

| Bullet added | Entries in `model_cost_manifest.json` (`provider` field) |
|---|---|
| **Meta**: Meta Muse models | 6 (`meta`) |
| **MiniMax**: MiniMax M2 and M3 models | 6 (`minimax`) |
| **Mistral AI**: Mistral Large, Medium, Small, Codestral, Devstral, Magistral, and other Mistral models | 47 (`mistralai`) |
| **Together AI**: Llama, Qwen, DeepSeek, GLM, Kimi, and other Together-hosted models | 78 (`together`) |
| **Z.ai**: GLM-4.x and GLM-5.x models | 14 (`zai`) |

I took each bullet's wording from the model names actually in the manifest, not from the providers' marketing pages. The provider keys match `PROVIDER_PREFIXES` in `scripts/cost_tracking/sync_models.py`.
